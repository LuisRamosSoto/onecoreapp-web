Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy applies to consumers in Washington State (under the My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373) and Nevada (under Senate Bill 370 of 2023), and to anyone else whose consumer health data OneCore handles. It is provided by Luis Arturo Ramos Soto, Mar Adriático 1256, Col. 3ra Burócrata, Ciudad Juárez, Chihuahua 32360, Mexico ("we"), the developer of the OneCore app. Our general Privacy Policy covers all other personal data.

1. Consumer health data we collect and why

  • Body weight and weight history: to show your progress and calculate DOTS strength points.
  • Progress photos (photos of your body): to let you compare your progress over time. Stored privately; only you can see them.
  • Sex (optional): only to calculate DOTS points.
  • Workout data (routines, workouts, sets, loads, reps, records, duration): to log and plan your training, show statistics and calculate points in training groups.
  • Data read from Apple Health (steps, distance, exercise minutes, workouts and weight): to show your weight and to automatically check off habits linked to Health. These readings are processed on your iPhone. The only items saved to your account are the weight you import with "Import from Health" and the record that a linked habit was completed.
  • Health-related habits you create (for example, "drink water" or "walk 8,000 steps") and their check-ins: to track your habits and streaks.

We use this data only to provide the features you request, keep the service secure and comply with the law. We never use it for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't use it to infer other health conditions.

2. Where it comes from

  • Directly from you, when you enter it or take photos in the app.
  • From Apple Health on your iPhone, only with your permission in iOS.
  • From your Apple Watch, when you log workouts with the OneCore Watch app.

3. Health data we share and with whom

  • Categories shared: body weight, progress photos, sex, workout data and habit check-ins (as stored data); and, in training groups, only derived results: points, totals and records.
  • Processors: Supabase (cloud database and file storage), which stores the data on our behalf under contract.
  • Apple: if you enable "Save to Health", workouts and focus minutes are written to Apple Health on your device.
  • Other users you choose: members of your training groups see your name, points, totals and group records, never your weight, sex, photos or set details.
  • Affiliates: we have none.
  • Authorities: only if required by law.

Receipt photos sent to OpenAI for AI reading are financial, not health, data. We do not share consumer health data with OpenAI.

Third-party tracking: no third party collects consumer health data about you over time or across websites or online services through OneCore. We don't use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

4. Consent

We ask for your consent before collecting health data that isn't strictly needed for a feature you request, and iOS asks separately before OneCore can read or write Apple Health data. We do not sell consumer health data, and we would ask for your separate consent before sharing it for any purpose not described here. You can withdraw consent at any time: delete the data in the app, turn off Health access in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › OneCore, or email us.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Confirm and access: know whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, get a copy of it, and receive a list of all third parties and affiliates with whom we've shared it, with their contact information.
  • Withdraw consent to our collection and sharing of your consumer health data.
  • Delete your consumer health data. We will also tell our processors to delete it. Copies in backups are deleted within six months.
  • Review and request changes: you can see and correct most of this data directly in the app.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

6. How to exercise your rights

  • In the app: edit or delete your weight, photos, sex and workouts directly, or delete your whole account in Settings › Delete account.
  • By email: write to contacto@onecoreapp.com.mx with the subject "Health data request", from the email linked to your account (or tell us which account it is). We may ask for more information to verify your identity; you won't need to create a new account.

We respond within 45 days of receiving your request. If we need more time, we may extend this once by another 45 days and will tell you why within the first 45 days. Requests are free up to twice a year.

Appeals: if we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or emailing us with the subject "Health data appeal" within a reasonable time. We'll respond in writing within 45 days, explaining our decision. If your appeal is denied, you can file a complaint with the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint or, in Nevada, with the Nevada Attorney General at ag.nv.gov.

7. Security

Health data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to your own account by row-level security, and progress photos are kept in private storage that only you can access.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in the app. If a change affects how we collect or share consumer health data, we'll ask for your consent before it applies.
