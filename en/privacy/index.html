Privacy Policy — OneCore

Last updated: November 3, 2026

OneCore is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that brings together tasks, habits, notes, projects, personal finances and workouts. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, on what legal basis, who it is shared with, how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights. It covers the app, its widgets and Watch app, and this website.

The short version: we don't sell your data, there are no ads and no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and we don't use third-party SDKs to measure what you do. What you create is stored on your phone and on our server so it can sync. Several things are processed only on your iPhone and never reach us (bank statements you import, Apple Health readings, dictation, Calendar). You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

  • Privacy and rights requests: contacto@onecoreapp.com.mx. The same person handles data requests and is the person in charge of the protection of personal information (including for Quebec law purposes).

2. Data we handle

We only handle what each feature needs to work. Here is what is stored on our server, by area:

Account

  • Name, email address and account ID. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email that service shares with us (Apple may give us a relay address that hides yours).
  • App preferences (language, currency, enabled modules, notifications).
  • Invitations: your invite code, who invited you and how many people joined with yours. There are no rewards or payments for inviting.

Tasks, habits, notes and projects

  • What you write: tasks, lists, notes, comments, events, countdowns, habits and their check-ins, projects, milestones, time tracked and files you attach.
  • Shared spaces and projects: who is a member, their role and the activity inside that space or project.

Finances

  • Accounts, cards, transactions, labels, budgets, savings goals, loans and their payments, installment purchases, tax-deductible items, and household finances shared with people you invite.
  • Receipt photos you attach to a transaction, stored as proof in a private area of your account.
  • Transactions you import from a bank statement or a screenshot. The statement file is read on your iPhone and never uploaded; only the transactions you confirm are saved.
  • We never ask for or store bank passwords, full card numbers or payment details. We don't connect to your bank.

Workouts

  • Routines, plans, workouts, sets, loads, reps and personal records.
  • Body weight, if you enter it or bring it in from Apple Health.
  • Progress photos, in a private area of your account that only you can see.
  • Sex (optional), used only to calculate your DOTS score.
  • Training groups: membership, points, league position, challenges, duels, reactions and nudges between members.

Notifications, purchases and support

  • Push notifications: your device's notification token (issued by Apple) and your notification preferences.
  • OneCore Pro: Apple confirms your purchase to us (product, dates, status, transaction ID and storefront country). We never see your card or payment details: Apple handles billing.
  • Support: the messages you send us and whatever you include in them.

Usage data (first-party analytics)

  • Simple events such as "opened the app" or "created a task", with the app version, linked to your account. They never include titles, text, amounts or photos. They are stored in our own database (Supabase); we don't use third-party analytics or advertising tools.
  • You can turn them off in Settings › Privacy › Share usage data (Settings opens from the profile icon on Home).

Website

  • Waitlist: if you leave your email, we store the email, the page you signed up from and whether you want to try beta versions.
  • The server hosting this site logs technical data for each visit (IP address, browser, page and time) to operate and protect against abuse. The site uses no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.

3. Sensitive data

Some data deserves extra care because misuse could harm you. We use it only for the feature you ask for, never for advertising or profiling, and it is not shown to other people except as explained below for groups.

  • Body weight and its history.
  • Progress photos (photos of your body).
  • Sex, if you choose to provide it for DOTS.
  • Apple Health data: steps, distance, exercise minutes, workouts and weight. These are read on your iPhone. The only Health data saved to your account is the weight you import with "Import from Health" and, if you link a habit to Health, the record that you completed it (the same as checking it off by hand).
  • Financial data: accounts, transactions, balances, loans, goals and receipt photos.

We ask for your explicit consent when you start using each of these features: when you enter your weight or sex, save your first progress photo and connect Health (through Apple’s permission). The first time you open Finances we explain what is saved. All of them are optional: you can use the rest of the app without them. You can delete all your body data at once in Settings › Privacy › Delete my body data, delete any single item in the app, or delete your whole account.

In training groups, other members see your name, points, totals and group records. They never see your weight, photos or the details of your sets. DOTS points are calculated from your weight and sex, and only the result is shared. If you gave your sex, your figure in the "head-to-head" comparison is drawn as a man or a woman, so whoever opens it can infer it; if you don’t want that, don’t give it or delete it in Settings › Privacy. Outside groups, no one else sees your sex.

4. What is processed on your phone and never reaches us

  • Bank statements (PDFs or screenshots) you import are read on your iPhone. Only the transactions you confirm are saved.
  • Apple Health: steps, minutes and workouts are read on your iPhone and not uploaded (except for weight and habits as described above). If you turn on "Save to Health", OneCore writes your workouts and focus minutes to Health, which stays on your device.
  • Dictation is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition. OneCore doesn't keep the audio, only the text you confirm.
  • Apple Calendar and Reminders are read on your phone to show them in OneCore. They are not uploaded.
  • Location-based reminders are triggered by iOS when you arrive at or leave a place. The app doesn't track your location, and the place is stored only on your phone.
  • Widgets, Apple Watch, Siri and Shortcuts use a copy on your device. You can hide amounts in widgets.
  • Backups (Pro): the file is created on your phone and you choose where to keep it.

5. Why we use your data

Primary purposes (needed to provide the service)

  • Creating and managing your account and signing you in.
  • Storing and syncing your content across your devices.
  • Running each module: tasks, habits, finances, workouts, projects, shared spaces and groups (including points and leagues).
  • Reading receipts with AI when you ask us to.
  • Sending the reminders and notifications you set up.
  • Knowing whether you have OneCore Pro, applying free-plan limits and verifying purchases with Apple.
  • Answering support messages and rights requests.
  • Keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and fraud, and meeting legal obligations.

Secondary purposes (not needed for the service)

  • Measuring, in aggregate, which parts of the app are used so we can improve it (first-party analytics).
  • Emailing you when the app or a beta becomes available, if you joined the waitlist.

How to opt out of secondary purposes: turn off Settings › Privacy › Share usage data, or email contacto@onecoreapp.com.mx asking us not to use your data for these purposes or to remove you from the waitlist. Opting out doesn't affect the service.

We don't make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Group points are a game score and don't affect your rights.

6. Consent and how to withdraw it

We ask for your permission before handling sensitive data, before sending your first receipt to AI and, where the law requires it, before analytics. iOS also asks your permission for Health, photos, camera, microphone, Calendar, location and notifications.

You can withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect processing that already happened):

  • AI receipt reading: Settings › Privacy › Read receipts with AI. When it's off, the photo is saved as proof without being read.
  • Usage data: Settings › Privacy › Share usage data.
  • Weight, progress photos and sex: Settings › Privacy › Delete my body data (your workouts stay). You can also delete individual weights or photos in Workouts.
  • Apple Health, photos, camera, location, notifications: in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security (or › Notifications).
  • Everything: delete your account in Settings › Delete account, or email us.

Withdrawing consent may switch off a feature (for example, no weight means no DOTS points), but the rest of the app keeps working.

7. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. It is only received by:

Service providers working on our behalf (processors)

They use the data only to provide their service to us, under contract and with security safeguards.

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore): database, file storage, sign-in and server functions. Server location: United States (Ohio, Amazon Web Services). Safeguards: its data processing agreement, with confidentiality and security commitments.
  • OpenAI (OpenAI OpCo, LLC, United States): only the receipt photos you ask us to read, to extract the amount, merchant and date. OpenAI processes them on the spot, doesn't use them to train its models and may keep them for up to 30 days to detect abuse before deleting them. Safeguards: its data processing agreement, with confidentiality and security commitments.
  • Hostinger (Hostinger International Limited, Cyprus): hosting for this website and its technical logs.

Companies acting on their own behalf

  • Apple (Apple Inc., US): Sign in with Apple, subscription billing, push notifications (APNs), Apple Health and speech recognition. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Google (Google LLC, US): only if you sign in with Google.

People you choose

  • Shared spaces and projects: members see the content of that space or project (including attachments and project money), your name and your email.
  • Household finances: household members see what is recorded in that household.
  • Training groups: members see your name, points, totals and group records (see section 3).

Authorities

Only when a law or an order from a competent authority requires it, and limited to what is requested.

International transfers

Because the service relies on providers outside your country (the United States and Singapore), your data may be processed in other countries, using the safeguards listed above. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards by email.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and content (including sensitive and financial data): for as long as you have an account. What you delete in the app is deleted from the server.
  • When you delete your account: your account and data are removed from the server immediately, including receipt photos, progress photos and attachments, as well as the data on your phone. If you signed in with Apple, OneCore's access to your Apple account is also revoked. Our provider's technical backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Usage data (analytics): 12 months from when it is generated.
  • Support messages: 24 months from the last message.
  • Photos sent to OpenAI: up to 30 days at OpenAI.
  • OneCore Pro purchase records: while you have an account; if tax law requires us to keep any income record, only that record and only for the legal period (in Mexico, generally 5 years under Article 30 of the Federal Tax Code).
  • Waitlist: until you ask to leave or, at most, 24 months after you signed up.
  • Website technical logs: kept for as long as the hosting provider keeps them for the site's security and operation; we don't use them for anything else.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

Depending on where you live, you can:

  • Access your data and learn how we use it.
  • Correct it if it's inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete it.
  • Object to its use or ask us to restrict its use or disclosure.
  • Portability: receive it in a structured format.
  • Withdraw consent (section 6).
  • Not be subject to solely automated decisions with significant effects (we don't make any).

In the app: almost everything is visible and you can edit or delete it at any time. To delete your account: Settings › Delete account, at the bottom of the screen (this can't be undone). With Pro you can export to CSV and PDF.

By email: write to contacto@onecoreapp.com.mx from your account email (or tell us which one it is) with your name, the right you want to exercise and the data it concerns, plus proof of authority if you act for someone else. If we can't verify your identity we'll ask for a little more information; we'll never ask you to create a new account. It's free. A full copy of your data is sent in a readable format (JSON or CSV).

Response times: we reply as soon as we can and always within the time your law allows: Canada and Quebec, 30 days; US (health data), 45 days; Mexico, 20 business days; Colombia, 10 business days for inquiries and 15 for claims; Argentina, 10 calendar days for access and 5 business days for correction or deletion; Chile, 30 calendar days (from December 1, 2026). If we refuse a request, we'll tell you why and how to challenge it.

10. Complaints and regulators

You can complain to us first: email contacto@onecoreapp.com.mx with the subject "Privacy complaint". We acknowledge receipt within 30 days (usually much sooner), look into it, keep you updated and tell you the outcome without undue delay. This doesn't stop you from going straight to your regulator:

  • Canada: Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC). In Quebec: Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI).
  • United States: your state Attorney General; in Washington, the Washington State Office of the Attorney General; in Nevada, the Nevada Attorney General.
  • Mexico: Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (the data protection authority for private-sector data since March 2025, replacing INAI).
  • Argentina: Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP).
  • Colombia: Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC).
  • Chile: Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales (once Law 21.719 takes effect); until then, the courts (Law 19.628).

11. Children

OneCore is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 to use it. In Quebec and Chile, users under 14 need the consent of a parent or guardian. We don't knowingly collect data from children under these ages; if you believe a child has given us their data, email us and we'll delete it.

12. Security and breach notification

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest on Supabase's infrastructure.
  • On the server, each account can only read and write its own data and what has been shared with it (row-level security). Private files (receipts, progress photos) live in storage areas only their owner can access.
  • Your session is stored in the iPhone Keychain. Purchases are verified with Apple's signature.
  • We don't write personal data to the app's technical logs.

No system is perfect. If a security breach significantly affects your rights, we'll tell you promptly by email or in the app, explaining what happened, what data was involved, what we did and what you can do. We'll also notify the competent authority where the law requires it (for example, the Commission d'accès à l'information in Quebec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada).
